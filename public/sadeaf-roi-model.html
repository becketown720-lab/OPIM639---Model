OPIM639 Defense Kit: Anticipated Professor Questions

Q1: Is a 40% AI triage deflection realistic for a vulnerable deaf organization?
Yes. The 40% baseline target isolates Tier-1 deterministic enquiries (sign language class schedules, venue accessibility, SingPass login assistance, and Video Relay Service / VRS setup), which constitute 60%+ of routine inbound messages. High-touch deaf social work and case management are strictly routed to human specialists.
Q2: Why does WCAG contrast failure equal S$ 92,000 in recovered donations?
SADeaf receives S$ 4.617M in tax-deductible cash donations. The current website features pale white text on bright yellow CTA buttons (< 3:1 contrast ratio, failing WCAG AA 4.5:1), leading to drop-offs among elderly patrons and low-vision donors. A conservative 2.0% conversion recovery unlocks S$ 92,340 in recaptured donations.
Q3: How does this solve the 61-staff paradox?
Course revenue surged +285% (S$ 2.083M) while staff plummeted from 80 to 61 (-23.8%). 960 hours of automated triage prevents staff burnout and avoids hiring expensive temporary agency caseworkers, freeing 0.5 FTE equivalent to focus on community advocacy.
